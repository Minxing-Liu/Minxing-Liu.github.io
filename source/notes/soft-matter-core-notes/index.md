<h2 id="Suggested-Review-Order">Suggested Review Order</h2><ol>
<li>Start with Introduction for the shared language: k_B T, mesoscopic scale, entropy, and fluctuations.</li>
<li>Read Fluid dynamics and Brownian Motion together to connect low-Reynolds-number flow, damping, and diffusion.</li>
<li>Use Elasticity, Polymers, and Liquid Crystals for solid deformation, chain conformation, and orientational order.</li>
<li>Use Soft Matter Phases and Interfaces/Membranes for phase behavior, self-assembly, surfaces, and curved membranes.</li>
</ol>
