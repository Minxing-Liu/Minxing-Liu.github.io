Advanced Statistical Mechanics

A continuously updated review base for the PhD qualifying exam path

This course is a core foundation for my current direction and is tied to the PhD qualifying exam. The practical target is clear: a final score above 82 can waive the qualifying exam requirement. This page is organized around concept closure, reproducible derivations, and live notes collected while reviewing lecture videos.

Review Roadmap

01 ensembles and potentials Partition functions, free energies, Legendre transforms, grand canonical ensemble.
02 fluctuation and response Fluctuation formulas, correlation functions, response functions, susceptibility.
03 phase transitions Order parameters, Landau theory, critical exponents, limits of approximation.
04 exam-sheet compression Rewrite the 53-page baseline into a genuinely usable pre-exam sheet.

Review Rules

  1. After each video segment, write down the exact point of confusion first.
  2. Attach one physical meaning and one validity condition to every important formula.
  3. Extract at least one typical problem shape or oral-exam question per chapter.
  4. Clearly separate borrowed baseline material from my own rewritten notes.
